Today feels a little surreal. After years of passing memories, I finally pinned down a place from my childhood: a cliff tucked away in the heart of Lapu-Lapu City. Using Google Maps with Earth mode turned on, I found it again. Not some coastal cliff everybody knows—this one's inland, larger, raw, and beautiful.

When I was a child, we used to go there. Below the cliff lies a shallow lake or swamp with light-colored sand or mud beneath. Thick vegetation wraps around the water. We used to catch colorful little fishes called "gapi" there — tiny splashes of life darting under the surface.

I visited it a second time years later, meeting up with friends. The cliff still had that quiet magic to it. It wasn't about the activities; it was the place itself — untouched, unbothered, and almost forgotten by time.

I'm planning to visit it again, properly this time. Camera ready, memories loaded. Maybe it’s time to name it officially on Google Maps, give it the recognition it deserves.

Here's to reliving old adventures, one forgotten cliff at a time.
